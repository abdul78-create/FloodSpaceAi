3. Mandatory Scientific Limitations & Attributions

Exposure vs. Confirmed Damage: All figures represent modeled potential inundation and spatial intersection with OpenStreetMap infrastructure footprints. They do not confirm physical destruction, structural collapse, or water ingress. In-situ engineering inspection is required before declaring structural losses.
Topological Disruption vs. Physical Isolation: Cut-off settlements represent vehicular graph disconnects. Footpaths, mule trails, river ferries, or aerial access routes may remain open.
Sensor Limitations & Cloud Cover: Post-event Sentinel-2 optical imagery had 75.9% mean cloud obscuration. Inundation is derived primarily from all-weather Sentinel-1 C-band synthetic aperture radar and remains optical-unconfirmed.
Independent Validation Isolation: Copernicus Emergency Management Service EMSR927 debris-flow footprints (1,068.36 ha, 1.01% IoU geometric overlap) are maintained strictly as an independent post-hoc co-location check isolated from pipeline inputs; this comparison is limited and cannot establish flood-detection accuracy by itself.
